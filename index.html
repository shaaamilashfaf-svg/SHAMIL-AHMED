01 Haus · Salon Booking App

Peaceful, Premium & Clean Self-Care at Home

Haus connects users with verified experts for hair, skincare, and wellness treatments at their doorstep. The platform offers effortless booking, flexible scheduling, and a convenient, stress-free self-care experience right in the comfort of home.

The UI is architected around soft soothing tones, elegant typography, and familiar home elements. Key booking screens were wireframed for maximum clarity, prominent calls to action, and seamless step-by-step flows.

Trust factors—such as specialist ratings, verified badges, and transparent pricing—were integrated at pivotal decision points, elevating accessibility and confidence for the end user.

Mobile App UX Figma Prototyping Design Systems Trust Badges Usability Testing

02 Gandaura · Natural Fragrance

Culturally Rooted Oud Luxury Experience

This project showcases a warm, culturally rooted, and user-focused digital experience for Gandaura, a natural artisanal fragrance brand. It creates an inviting platform for perfume connoisseurs to discover authentic oud-inspired creations with effortless ease.

The design seamlessly blends traditional Middle Eastern craftsmanship with a clean, contemporary aesthetic. The visual hierarchy features structured olfactory pyramids (Top, Heart, and Base notes), high-resolution bottle presentation, and ingredient transparency.

The site architecture prioritizes smooth, distraction-free purchasing across Home, Collections, Fragrance Details, and Checkout, turning luxury browsing into an immersive ritual.

Luxury E-Commerce Web Design Olfactory Pyramids Visual Hierarchy Ingredient Transparency

03 RentBiz · Property SaaS

Clarity & Automation for Modern Landlords

A clean, modern, and efficiency-driven SaaS platform designed for landlords and property managers. RentBiz focuses on task automation and scannability, creating an approachable interface that communicates trust, simplicity, and high productivity.

By pairing a soft, warm color palette with minimal typography and rounded modular components, the platform eliminates cognitive clutter while handling complex property analytics, occupancy ratios, cashflow charts, and maintenance workflows.

Every core tool—from dashboard overviews to multi-unit tenant management—is organized into scannable cards that allow managers to make confident decisions in seconds.

SaaS Dashboard Modular Cards Data Visualization Information Architecture Complex Workflows

06 The Person Behind

The Person Behind

Language, Philosophy & Digital Empathy

I am Shamil Ashfaf, a UI/UX designer based in Payyoli, Kozhikode. What fundamentally shapes my design thinking is an unconventional blend of disciplines: a background in English Literature combined with deep studies in Islamic Fundamentals and Ethics.

"Design is an act of deep listening. Before touching a single pixel, we must understand the human being on the other side of the screen."

Literature taught me the weight of language, the subtleties of human psychology, and how narrative clarity eliminates anxiety in digital products. Islamic fundamentals cultivated an uncompromising discipline of intentionality (Niyyah), balance, ethical consideration, and patience.

When designing in Figma, I don't see mere cards, grids, or fleeting visual trends. I see people trying to accomplish meaningful goals—booking a self-care specialist, ordering an artisanal fragrance, or managing properties without overwhelm.

Rooted in the calm coastal rains of Kozhikode, I design digital products that are thoughtful, enduring, and genuinely respectful of human attention.
